Privacy Policy

This policy describes how we treat information when you use our mobile application. We keep it short and transparent.

Last updated March 25, 2026

Overview

We do not collect personal data. No user-specific data is collected or stored by us through this app for profiling, marketing, or analytics purposes as described below.

Information we collect

We do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personally identifiable information for storage on our servers in connection with ordinary app use.

If you contact us by email, we will receive only what you choose to send in that message.

Sharing with third parties

No data is shared with third parties for advertising or sale. We do not sell your personal information.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have received information from a child, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps, including deletion where required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
